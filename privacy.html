Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · Lockfy for Android and iOS (com.abdullahwins.LockInfoSlap) · NADESOFT

In short: Lockfy does not collect, share or sell any personal data. The app makes no network requests. Your Medical ID, your design and your photo are created and kept on your own device, and we never see them.

1. Who we are

Lockfy is published by NADESOFT ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the Lockfy app for Android and iOS handles information. Questions? Email contact@nadesoft.bd.

2. Information you enter

To build your lock screen you can enter a Medical ID: your name, date of birth, organ donor status, up to three emergency contacts (name, relationship and phone number), blood type, allergies, medical conditions, medications and notes.

  • It is stored only in the app's private storage on your device, so it's there next time you open Lockfy.
  • It is used only to draw your lock screen card, and the optional QR code, on your device.
  • It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

Your lock screen is public by nature. Anyone holding your phone can read what's on it, and anyone who scans the QR code can save the contacts it contains. Show only what a helper needs, and leave out addresses, ID numbers and other sensitive details.

3. Photos

  • If you use your own photo as a background, you choose it with the system photo picker. Lockfy receives only the single photo you pick, not your photo library.
  • A copy of that photo is kept in the app's private storage so your design keeps working. Older background photos are deleted when you replace them.
  • Photos are processed on your device only and are never uploaded.

4. Saving and setting your wallpaper

  • When you save, Lockfy adds the finished image to your photo library. That is the only reason it asks for permission to add photos. On iOS this is add-only access.
  • Android: when you tap Set as lock screen, Lockfy uses the system wallpaper service on your device (the SET_WALLPAPER permission). If that isn't available, it opens the system wallpaper chooser instead.
  • iOS: you set the saved image yourself from the Photos app.
  • If you tap Share, the image goes only to the app or person you choose in the system share sheet.

5. Review reminders

If you turn on the optional review reminder, Lockfy schedules a local notification on your device every 90 days. It is created and delivered entirely on the device. No push service, device token or server is involved. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

6. Data we collect or share

None. Lockfy:

  • makes no network requests and works fully offline;
  • does not collect personal, device, usage or location data;
  • contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking software;
  • does not share or sell data to anyone;
  • needs no account or sign-in;
  • does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

In store terms: the Google Play Data safety section says "No data collected" and "No data shared", and the App Store privacy label says "Data Not Collected".

7. Purchases

Lockfy is bought through Google Play or the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple, and we never receive your payment details. Their handling of that information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

8. Children

Lockfy is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from children. A parent can use Lockfy to make a safety lock screen for a child's device, and that information stays on the device as described above.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Your Medical ID, design, photo and settings stay on your device until you remove them. To delete them:

  • in Lockfy, open Settings → Erase all data. This deletes your Medical ID, your design and its photo, and cancels reminders;
  • or clear the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the app.

Wallpapers you already saved to your photo library are kept until you delete them there. We hold no copy, so there's nothing for us to keep, disclose or delete on your behalf.

10. Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold personal data about you, there's no data for us to access, correct, export or erase. You stay in full control of everything on your device, under the GDPR, the CCPA and similar laws. You can still contact us with any privacy question.

11. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log basic request data such as IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, we'll also say so in the app's release notes.
