Getting started

How do I make my lock screen?
  1. Open the Medical ID tab and fill in your details. You only do this once.
  2. On Home, tap the palette button to pick a theme, or your own photo, and choose what the card shows.
  3. Tap Set as lock screen.
AndroidHow do I set it as my lock screen?

Tap Set as lock screen. Lockfy applies it directly; you can also choose Set on lock and home screens.

Some devices don't let apps change the wallpaper directly. In that case Lockfy opens the system wallpaper chooser, or you can tap Share and open the image with your Gallery or Photos app, then use its "Set as wallpaper" option.

iPhoneHow do I set it as my lock screen?

iOS doesn't allow apps to change your wallpaper, so Lockfy saves the image to Photos at your screen's full resolution. Then:

  1. Open Photos and find the wallpaper you just saved.
  2. Tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper.
  3. Tap Add and choose Set as Lock Screen.

Tip: turn off "Depth Effect" on the lock screen if the clock overlaps your card.

Can I edit the text or move the card around?

No, and that's on purpose. Lockfy builds the card from your Medical ID and places it below the clock and widgets, so it stays readable on every design. You choose the theme, card style, accent colour, background and which details are shown.

Your card

The clock or widgets cover part of my card

Lock screens differ between phones. Try showing fewer details (Customize → Card) or fewer contacts. On iPhone, also try turning off Depth Effect and removing large widgets.

My photo makes the card hard to read

In Customize → Background, raise Darken (35% or more works well for bright photos) and add some Blur. Lockfy shows a warning when a photo is too bright.

What does the QR code contain?

A contact card with your emergency contacts' names and phone numbers. Anyone who scans it can save them to their phone. It's generated on your device and works without internet. You can turn it off in Customize → Card.

Can the card show two languages?

Yes. In Customize → Card, choose a second language. Labels such as "Blood type" and "Allergy" then appear in English and that language. 20 languages are available.

Home says my lock screen is out of date

You changed your Medical ID or design after setting the lock screen. Tap Update lock screen to apply the new version.

How do review reminders work?

Turn on Review reminder in Settings to get a notification every 90 days asking you to check your details. It's scheduled on your device, with no account or server involved. If it doesn't appear, check that notifications are allowed for Lockfy in your system settings.

Your data

Where is my information stored?

Only on your phone. Lockfy makes no network requests and has no account, so nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

I got a new phone. Can I move my data?

Because nothing is stored online, your Medical ID isn't synced. Install Lockfy on the new phone and enter your details again; it takes about a minute. Wallpapers you saved to Photos move with your photo library.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings → Erase all data. This deletes your Medical ID, your design and its photo, and cancels reminders. Wallpapers already saved to Photos are kept until you delete them there.

Purchases

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us:

  • AndroidGoogle Play: open play.google.com → Account → Order history, or see Google Play's refund help.
  • iPhoneApp Store: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose "Request a refund".
Do I need to buy it again on another device?

On the same Google or Apple account, you can install it on your other devices without paying again. Android and iPhone are separate purchases.
